# Project Scoping — Sector Rotation Signal

Portfolio managers rebalancing across sector ETFs with tech, energy, healthcare, etc. currently rely on intuition , feeling or news to decide which sectors are the best to invest. This creates the risk of buying into a sector after its momentum has already faded.

This project builds a simple monthly signal that ranks sector ETFs by risk-adjusted
momentum. The stakeholder is the Portfolio Manager who owns the rebalancing decision,
the useful answer is a ranked list delivered before each monthly rebalance, meant to
inform not replace the Portfolio Manager judgment.
